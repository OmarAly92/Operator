# After 2B.1: what is still to do

Date: 2026-10-06, after the review fix wave (parts 1 and 2). Status: **TODO, not planned yet.** These are the residuals of 2B.1 (branch `feat/ios-liquid-glass-2b1`, motion measured at `36039801b`). Every number and its run folder is in `results-2b1.md`. Saved tool outputs are under `research/execution-2b1/` (paths relative to this folder); an item whose evidence is "Task N review", a P1-n finding or a P2-n ruling is a line of `research/execution-2b1/ledger.md`. The fit is `research/execution-2b1/fix2-fit.json`.

Where 2B.1 ended: Done items 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 pass; item 4 passes 136 of 168 progress measures in normal mode and 133 of 168 under Reduce Motion, with the event and touch gates 120 of 120. The 67 failures are classed in `results-2b1.md` (groups G1–G6): 39 (b), 28 (a).

When an item becomes work, it follows ROADMAP §5 (prototype on the iOS 27 simulator, a plan with the tested code, a fresh session, review here) and never loosens a limit or a measure. Classes: **(a)** tunable, **(b)** model limitation, **(c)** lab scene, **(d)** measurement or harness, **(e)** debug build; **perf** for M10 and **defect** for package or harness behaviour found in review. Rows marked **fixed** are kept so the history reads in one place.

## Belongs to 2B.2 (M4)

- **Container spacing animation** moved here (ruling 29): a `spacing` change jumps in 2B.1, because 2B.2 changes what `spacing` means.
- **Native's merge reach is about half its `spacing`** (ruling 23), like the package's smooth-min, not spec M4's "closer than `spacing`". Native N7 stills (run `20261004-003527`, dark-photo): the default container joins at gaps 0 and 4 pt (necks 25.33 and 4.00 pt) and keeps 8 pt and wider apart; `spacing: 40` joins at 0–20 pt (necks 50.67, 38.33, 40.67, 34.00, 24.67 and 2.00 pt) and keeps 24 pt and wider apart.
- An appearing glass does not merge with its neighbours until it settles (ruling 18); decide from native morph frames whether that matters.

## Facts, no action

- **Native's default spring** fits 0.57–0.59 s / 0.94–1.01 in every recording group (0.58 / 0.97 on the fresh-boot data), consistent with SwiftUI's 0.55 / 1.0 within the fit's resolution: the error surface is flat (A3). The lab's progress measure gives systematically different springs per backdrop (dark-stripes 0.69–0.73 s, light-stripes 0.44–0.46 in every group). The presets stay SwiftUI's springs (ruling 10).
- **The 250 × 44 press** grows +17.00 to +17.33 pt on `photo` (N2 and its noise takes), the spike's +17.67 within 0.67 pt. On `stripes` L1 reads +14.33: both capsule ends sit in its blind band at the peak, which cost 2.7–3.0 pt of growth there. The size law fitted on `photo` is Δw = min(16.82, 1014 / h) pt, RMS 0.67 pt; 2B.3 judges press against it.
- **The slow first noise session** (appear about 12 ms slower, presses held 3.26 s) came from a simulator boot from before the 2026-10-04 reboot, not from load. After a fresh boot every native press reads 0.98–1.00 s.
- **Native's appear overshoot** per backdrop (noise takes and N5/N6): `.bouncy` 1.80–2.80 % normal and 3.53–4.09 % under Reduce Motion, lowest on dark-photo; `.snappy` 0.28–0.30 % and 0.37–0.48 %.
