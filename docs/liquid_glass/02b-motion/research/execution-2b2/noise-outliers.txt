Task 25 Step 5: measures whose noise is above 3 x the median of the others, per case (gotcha 52)

Source: read-only pass over build/glass_lab/runs/noise-2b2, the ten pair result.json files of each of the 62 cases (the values lab.case_noise takes the maximum of). For every case and every measure the maximum over the ten pairs equals the value in tool/glass_lab/noise.json (0 differences), so each worst value below is that case's noise.

Definitions
- outlier: a measure whose worst pair value is above 3 x the median of the other nine pairs' values.
- median 0: 3 x 0 is 0, so any positive worst would count. Such a measure is skipped when its worst is below its fixed threshold in metrics.py (2046 measure-cases skipped) and listed when its worst is at or above it (46 listed, shown as med0 in the ratio column).
- drivers: every pair above 3 x the median is an outlier pair. 'take N in K pairs' means take N is in all K outlier pairs. With K = 1 the pair names two takes and the likelier one is the take whose other three pairs have the higher mean (both means given); 'indistinguishable' means those means are equal. 'no common take' means the outlier pairs share no take.
- sets limit: limit with = max(fixed, 1.5 x worst); limit without = max(fixed, 1.5 x the largest value among the non-outlier pairs). The outlier sets the limit when with > without; both limits are printed. The outlier measures of one take are not independent: removing that take would lower all of them together.

Why they stay (gotcha 52)
Gotcha 52 moved a take because it had a measured fault, a disappear onset 98 ms before the glass moved. Here no take has a measured fault (Task 25 Step 5 Ruling in ledger.md, noise-take-check.txt): all 40 morph takes have 2 touches and no zero-length touch window, no animation is squeezed, and the long first-frame gap that take_check flags is the still screen before the tap, which passing takes show too; respace windows are 25-74 ms with count 2; union and spacing have no touch steps and all their static mad are 0.0. The values below are therefore spread of sound takes in time, damping, overshoot, response and sharpness measures that the video reads in 8.3 ms steps (peak_ms and settle_ms values are multiples of 8.33), not a capture fault. They stay because no excluding criterion applies to the driving takes, and because choosing which of five takes to drop by one measure would pick the floor by the number instead of by the recording. The cost is real and is stated: 754 of the 841 outliers set a limit, so those limits are looser than the other takes alone would give (the with and without limits show by how much), and the counts in the Done table are judged under them. Reversible: recompute noise.json from the takes with any take excluded (R/noise_recompute.py).
