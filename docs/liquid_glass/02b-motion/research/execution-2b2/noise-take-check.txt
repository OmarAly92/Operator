# Task 25f: why take_check flags material.morph and material.respace (read-only analysis)

Reran `take_check.py` on all 40 `material.morph` and 10 `material.respace` takes of `build/glass_lab/runs/noise-2b2` (one process per take, `--scene` the scene id). The 50 JSON lines are identical to the earlier run kept in the session scratchpad (`tc1.txt`): 28 of 40 morph takes FAIL, 9 of 10 respace takes FAIL. Extra numbers below come from `shapes.capture` rows of the same takes (helper scripts in the session scratchpad only). No recording, no deletion, no commit, nothing written to the repo except this file.

## Bottom line

- No morph take has the gotcha 47 fault. All 40 have exactly 2 touches, none has a zero-length window (shortest 45 ms, median 77, longest 166 ms), and no animation is squeezed (10-90% of the stack height is 252-373 ms at step 1 and 150-325 ms at step 3, the same for PASS and FAIL takes of one case; one slower outlier at 535 ms).
- The failures come from the tap branch of the rule, `tap and hole and burst >= 2`, which does not use `zero`. It fires on a pattern that passing takes show too: a long frame gap before the tap (the still screen) followed by 1.7-5 ms frames. Only the count of those short gaps (1 in passing takes, 1-4 in failing ones) separates PASS from FAIL.
- The earlier report said all 28 failures are capture holes. It is 27 takes with a capture hole and 1 take (`light-stripes-reduce-motion/1`) with "touch step 3 owns no event".
- `material.respace` is a measure problem, not a capture problem: native animates the pair (2 components to 1 about 200 ms after the tap, neck 12 to 33 over 24-25 video frames, and back over 13-16 frames), but the tracker's event detection and significance test cannot see it.
- `material.union` and the 23 `material.spacing.*` scenes have no steps; all their static `ready.mad` and `settled.mad` in `noise.json` are 0.0.

## (2) Is the gap1 / burst signature in passing takes too? Yes, with the same values

First event of each step with `gap1_ms > 30`:

| step | verdict | takes with gap1 > 30 | gap1 range | burst values (count of takes) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 3 | FAIL | 23 of 27 | 2286.7-3036.7 ms | 0 (1), 1 (3), 2 (13), 3 (5), 4 (1) |

- gap1 is the same in PASS and FAIL takes: step 1 about 375-420 ms; step 3 about 2.3-2.4 s in normal mode (14 takes, 2287-2420 ms) and 2.5-3.0 s in Reduce Motion (12 takes, 2528-3037 ms). gap2 right after the stall is 1.7-5.0 ms in the passing takes with a stall (median 2.5 ms at step 1, 3.3 ms at step 3, max 6.7), the same as in the failing ones (median 3.3 ms). A passing take stops at one short gap; a failing one has two to four.
- The take with 0 events at step 3 and the low-gap takes are not part of the table above: 14 of 40 step-1 takes (4 PASS, 10 FAIL) have gap1 under 30 ms (11.7-18.3 ms, one 3.3 ms), a frame happened to land just before the tap.
- Origin: the first frame of the clip is the last still frame before the tap (in `dark-photo/1` the first frame is at 15.65 s and the onset at 16.047 s, a 397 ms gap) and the recording only writes a frame when the screen changes, so the still screen before a tap reads as a long gap1. At step 3 the same effect spans the wait between the two taps (tap-to-tap median 3.72 s in morph, one take 6.39 s, against 3.0 s in morph.plain; why morph waits longer is not known). The touch window is not squeezed in these takes (45-166 ms), and the onset equals the touch-down time in most of them (the marker and the first glass change land in the same frame).
- After the 1-4 short frames the frames return to the display rate: frame gaps 3 to 6 of the first step-1 event are 16.7 ms median in both groups (PASS min 3.3 max 16.7, FAIL min 3.3 max 20.0). Gotcha 47's fault would keep frames 1.7-6.7 ms apart through the animation.
- Why merge and morph.plain pass: merge first events have gap1 17-53 ms (39 of 40 above 30 ms but under 54) and burst 0-1; morph.plain step 1 has gap1 5-28 ms in 37 of 40 takes (the other 3 are 93-835 ms with burst 0-1) and step 3 gap1 20-100 ms, burst 0-1. Neither has a 380 ms or 2.3 s stall. Why the glass morph scene idles that long before a tap when plain and merge do not is not known; I did not find it in the data.

Whether to keep them, relax the tap branch (for example require `zero`, as gotcha 47 describes, or a squeezed 10-90), or gate morph with a timing check like the one above is the main session's decision.

## (4) material.respace: why no event for the taps

What the scene tracks: `scenes.json` `material.respace` has `track: ["pair"]`, `topology: ["pair"]`, region `[103, 399, 196, 104]`, steps wait, tap widen, wait, tap narrow, wait. The lab scene (`tool/glass_lab/native/GlassLab/MaterialScenes.swift:230`) is two 80 pt circles 12 pt apart in a `GlassEffectContainer(spacing: wide ? 40 : 8)`: the layout never changes, only the blend spacing, so the outer bounding box of the pair is constant.

How events are assigned (`shapes.capture`, `tool/glass_lab/harness/shapes.py:113-121`): an event is a run of frames whose mean absolute difference from the previous frame (whole region, downscaled by the pixel scale) exceeds `align.MOTION_THRESHOLD` 0.5; it is kept only if `significant(series)` holds, which tests a `count` change of at least 1 or a change of at least `MIN_EVENT_CHANGE` in width, height, cx, cy (4.0), luma (1.5) or progress (0.1); neck and gap are not in that test. `touch.owner` then gives the event to the last step whose touch started before its onset.

What the frames do (rows of `shapes.capture`, per take):

- Widen (step 1): the gap between the circles goes 10.7, 10.0, 8.7, 7.3, 3.0 over about 8 frames, then at +190 to +218 ms after touch-down the pair becomes one component and the neck grows 12 to 33 (dark) or 12.7 to 35.3 (light), in harness units, over 24-25 video frames (16.29 s to about 16.8 s in `dark-photo/0`). Narrow (step 3): the reverse, count 1 to 2 at +225 to +247 ms with 13-16 frames of changing neck. In all 10 takes. Native does animate the pair (neck over far more than 3 video frames), and the capture is sound: 2 touches per take, windows 25-74 ms, 16-18 ms frame gaps and burst 0 in every event that was found.
- Why it is not an event: bounding box width, height, cx, cy have 0.0 peak-to-peak in every window. The per-frame mean difference peaks at 0.507 in `dark-photo/0` (one frame, so align finds a one-frame event at 16.29-16.307 s and `significant` then drops it), 0.475 in `dark-photo/2` (below 0.5, no event at all), 0.545 and 0.611 in `light-photo/0`. Luma peak-to-peak in the 0.8 s after a tap is 0.2-0.3 in the dark takes (under 1.5) and 2.0 in the light takes (over 1.5), so a light take gets an event only when the 0.5 threshold also fires: events found for step 3 only in `light-photo/0` and `/1`, for steps 1 and 3 in `/4`, none in `/2` and `/3`, none in the 5 dark takes. The neck peak-to-peak in the same windows is 20-30 (widen) and 24-31 (narrow), present but not tested.
- Class: measure/manifest (event detection and the significance test never look at the neck or gap of a scene whose only motion is topology), not a capture fault. Consequence already in `noise.json`: `material.respace` `dark-photo` has no motion floor at all and `light-photo` only `step3e0` floors (`topology.neck_rms` 18.0, `split_ms` 58.3, `gap_rms` 1.80, `delay_ms`) from pairs that both found a step 3 event.
- take_check on respace (rerun): `dark-photo/0-4` FAIL, steps 1 and 3 own no event; `light-photo/0` and `/1` FAIL on step 1 only (step 3 event with gap1 16.7 and 15.0 ms, burst 0); `/2` and `/3` FAIL on both steps; `/4` PASS (events at steps 1 and 3, gap1 18.3 and 16.7 ms, burst 0). No respace take has a capture hole or a zero-length window.

Every one is 0.0 (max 0.0), so every one is at most 1.0. The Task 25c/d logs also print "5 takes, static mad 0.00" for each case.
