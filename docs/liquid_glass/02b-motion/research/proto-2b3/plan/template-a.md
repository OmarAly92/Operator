**Architecture.**
- **Lab.** `press.py` reads, per touch step and per tracked glass, the rows `shapes.capture` already makes plus one new value, `glow`: the mean luma inside the glass's rest box (or a pinned rect). From them it derives thirteen press measures (`press.glow_peak`, `glow_rise_ms`, `glow_fade_ms`, `glow_drop`, `glow_drop_ms`, `glow_level`, `glow_half_ms`, `grow_w`, `grow_h`, `grow_ms`, `shrink_ms`, `stretch`, `centre`), judged at max(fixed threshold, 1.5 × noise). `takecheck.py` finds a take's capture holes, squeezed presses and missing touches; `run` re-records such a take and `repeat` keeps it out of every floor. `pressfit.py` fits the package's press numbers from clean native series and writes `ios27_press.dart`; nothing is typed.
- **Package.** Each `GlassMember` owns a `GlassPress`: a growth spring, a swell spring for the light under the finger, a focus that follows the finger, and an even "hold" light that goes in one frame at a time that depends on the glass's size. The coordinator samples it on its ticker with the member's other springs, so a press rebuilds no widget. The member's drawn rect and its content scale together about the centre by the size law. An interactive `GlassEffect` puts a `GlassPressTarget` (raw pointer events, hit-tested against the glass's shape, never in the gesture arena) around its content and paints the light with `GlassPressGlow` under the content.
- **Example.** The N2 sizes, Operator's two sizes, the N3 drags and N4 neighbours as live scenes with native's tap ids; `perf.motion` and `perf.slide`.
- **Operator.** `GlassSurface(pressable: true)` becomes `Glass.interactive()`; `_PressLift` and its `GlassGlow` go (Task 10, separable, D9).

*Lab (spec L4)*

1. **The press measures are read per touch step on the glass's rest box** (Task 1, `press.py`, tests `test_press.py`). For each touch step of a scene and each tracked glass: the rest is the last frame before touch-down; `glow` is the mean luma inside the rest box (the box `track.box_pixels` finds in `ready.png` against the bare frame) or inside the region's pinned rect (ruling 4); `glow_peak` is its largest rise during the touch; `glow_rise_ms` the time from touch-down to 90 % of that peak; `glow_fade_ms` the time from touch-up until it stays within 10 % of the peak; `glow_drop` the largest fall between two consecutive frames after touch-up, `glow_drop_ms` when it happens and `glow_level` the light just before it (the level the fade reached, F13); `glow_half_ms` the time from touch-up to halfway between the peak and that level (or rest, without a cut), so the fade itself is judged and not only the cut (F13: `glow_fade_ms` equals `glow_drop_ms` whenever there is a cut); `grow_w` and `grow_h` the largest growth of the box during the touch; `grow_ms` the time to 90 % of `grow_w`; `shrink_ms` the time from touch-up until the width stays within max(10 % of `grow_w`, 2/3 pt); `stretch` the signed largest change of width/height against rest from touch-down to the next touch; `centre` the largest move of the box centre in that span. Thresholds: luminance 3 for `glow_peak`, `glow_drop` and `glow_level`, 17 ms for the times, 1 pt (`bbox_pt`) for `grow_w`/`grow_h`, a new `ratio` 0.01 for `stretch`, 1 pt (`centre_pt`) for `centre` (spec L4's numbers). **A shape that does not light (peak under 3 luma) or grow (under 1 pt) has no time measures; both apps without one agree (0), one app without one fails (inf).** That is how "absence where native shows none" is judged (spec §8 2B.3 item 2). **It reproduces the spike** (`R/reproduce-glow.txt`, `reproduce.py glow`): v13 dark-stripes first press: rise 35.0 ms (spike 35), fade 403.3 ms (spike 403), growth +12.0 / +4.67 pt (spike +12 / +4.67), growth to 90 % 210 ms (spike 210); the one-frame drop 11.23 luma at 403.3 ms; light-photo luma +19.00 / +19.05 (spike +19.04); N1 dark-photo +20.61 with its drop 11.39 at 400.0 ms.

*How the prototype's numbers were taken*

16. **The take check (F1, R1, S1) looks at each touch's whole span, and judges lost frames everywhere except inside native's own 20 Hz cadence** (`takecheck.span_holes`, tests in `test_takecheck.py` and `test_press.py`). The span runs from a touch-down to the next touch, or to 1.2 s after the last touch-up. A take has a capture hole when:
   - **a squeezed first frame**: the first frame after a touch-down is under 4.5 ms after it (where clean takes read 5.0–6.7 ms; a squeezed rise reads 5–25 ms early, the re-review's table);
   - **a burst**: two consecutive frame gaps under 4.5 ms (3.3 ms or less on the recording's 1/600 s clock) anywhere in the span, the cut included; native's release cadence alternates single short and long gaps (13.3, 15.0, 16.7, 3.3, 11.7, 5.0 ms) and never has two short ones in a row, which is how it is told apart from a burst, and why 2B.1's excluded `dark-photo` take, whose release is that cadence, passes;
