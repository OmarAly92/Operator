## Review Focus

1. **The press measures judge absence as well as presence, and a holed step is never a pass.** A glass that lights or grows in one app and not the other fails its time measures (inf); both quiet agree (0); a step with a capture hole reads its times as NaN, which fails and drops out of every floor. Pinned by `test_absent_times_on_both_sides_agree_and_on_one_side_fail`, `test_a_burst_after_touch_down_marks_the_step_and_its_times_are_judged_as_absent` and `test_a_press_measure_with_no_touch_window_is_absent_and_fails` (Task 1).
2. **The take rule** (ruling 16): over the whole span from touch-down to the next touch, a squeezed first frame, a burst of two short gaps (the cut included), or frames lost while the glass moves (for native, inside the windows where it always draws at 60 Hz) is a hole; native's alternating release cadence and its slow cadence after the onset are not. Pinned by `test_a_burst_anywhere_from_touch_down_to_the_next_touch_is_a_capture_hole` (a burst at +450 ms, the cut), `test_the_archived_dark_stripes_release_is_a_hole_with_its_lost_frames_and_its_burst` (2B.1's excluded take's gap list), `test_native_s_slow_cadence_after_its_onset_is_not_lost_and_the_same_gaps_in_flutter_are`, `test_a_gap_while_the_glass_moves_in_native_s_onset_is_lost`, `test_a_gap_after_a_still_frame_or_with_no_frame_after_it_in_the_span_is_not_lost`, `test_the_release_cadence_of_alternating_short_and_long_gaps_is_not_a_hole` and `test_a_first_frame_squeezed_onto_the_touch_down_frame_is_a_hole` (`test_takecheck.py`, Task 3, on Task 1's `takecheck.py`); `run`'s re-record and `repeat`'s exclusion and leverage stop by `CheckedRecordingTests`, `LeverageStopTests` and `LeverageTests`.
3. **Glow first, growth second, one uniform scale on the settled law in height and width, the cut per size.** Pinned by `a press lights the glass at once and grows it after the growth delay, uniformly, to the size law` and `on release the glow stays until the cut and drops to nothing in one frame` (Task 5), and in `pressfit` by `test_the_law_is_fitted_on_the_settled_growth_so_a_spring_overshoot_is_not_counted_twice`, `test_the_law_reads_every_take_s_plateau_holed_or_not`, `test_a_law_in_width_and_height_is_recovered_on_portrait_and_landscape_sizes`, `test_a_form_that_misses_a_measured_size_by_more_than_the_size_threshold_is_not_chosen`, `test_a_refit_where_no_form_keeps_every_size_within_the_threshold_writes_nothing`, `test_the_glow_profile_is_the_median_of_its_takes` and `test_the_cut_is_read_per_size_and_a_size_without_one_takes_its_nearest` (Task 4).
4. **Touches and lifecycle.** The eleven touch and lifecycle tests of `glass_press_test.dart` (ruling 11) and Operator's restored behaviour tests (ruling 15).
5. **Tool-written numbers, and exactly what `pressfit` guards.** `ios27_press.dart` equals `pressfit.py fit --write`'s output (Task 5's check) and is rewritten only by the tool (Task 14). `problems()` refuses to write when: no growth law form keeps every measured size within the 1 pt size threshold (test `test_a_refit_where_no_form_keeps_every_size_within_the_threshold_writes_nothing`: the table is left untouched); the chosen law is on its grid edge (cap 14–20 pt, growth at 100 pt 4–40 pt, height power 0.5–2, width power −1 to 1); sigma is on its grid edge (20–300 pt); more than 25 % of the per-take spring fits (growth, release, light rise, light fade) sit on their grid edge (response 0.01–1.5 s, damping 0.1–4); the Reduce Motion cut rests on fewer than five presses; any of the growth, release, rise, hold, fade, cut or Reduce Motion cut fits is missing; or there is no size table. **Not guarded:** the hold, swell and cut per size are medians with no minimum count (one clean take sets a size's row; `cases` in `pressfit.json` says how many), and among the forms that keep every size within 1 pt the lower leave-one-size-out RMS chooses, even when two forms are not separated (`law.separated` says so; ruling 8). Pinned by `test_problems_name_spring_fits_on_the_grid_edge_and_a_thin_reduce_motion_cut` and `test_write_refuses_a_summary_with_problems_and_writes_one_without`.
6. **What is not built and why**: no glow spreading and no container glow layer (ruling 4), no release while the finger is down (ruling 3), no lean toward a far finger (the user's D6 A; Task 14b is not to run), a white light (D10). A reviewer checks that no code path draws light on a neighbour or deforms the glass on a drag.
7. **Not pinned, said:** the light's look (the plus-mode paint cannot be read in `flutter test`, which draws `FakeGlass`, gotcha 39); the press-drag cut's cause (ruling 7); portrait glass growth (ruling 8); the light between and beyond the six sizes (ruling 6); the scroll takeover against native (ruling 11); whether the light follows the finger under Reduce Motion (ruling 10).
