### D6's lean toward a far finger: Task 14b's sketch (never prototyped, not to run in 2B.3)

Files it would touch: `tool/glass_lab/harness/pressfit.py` (a `lean` command, `fit_lean`, three constants in the table it writes) and `tests/test_pressfit.py`; `packages/ios_liquid_glass/lib/src/motion/glass_press.dart` (a lean spring), `glass_press_widgets.dart` (the pointer's position against the shape; `debugGlassPress` also returns the lean), `lib/src/liquid_glass.dart` (the drawn rect stretched and moved by the lean), `ios27_press.dart` (by `pressfit.py lean --write` only), `test/motion/glass_press_test.dart`.

**The model.** While a press's finger is outside the glass's shape, at a distance `d` pt from the shape along the unit direction `u` from the glass's centre to the finger, the glass's centre moves toward the finger by `S x (1 - exp(-d / L))` pt and its width-to-height ratio changes by `E x (1 - exp(-d / L))` along `u` (wider for a horizontal `u`, taller for a vertical one, area kept), on the growth spring; inside the shape, and under Reduce Motion, there is no lean. `S`, `E` and `L` are fitted on the clean `material.drag.{out,up,far}` takes (`stretch` and `centre` of each take against the finger's distance outside the shape, signed by axis), over a grid with an edge guard as the law's (`problems()` names a lean on its grid edge).

1. Fit tests in `test_pressfit.py`: `test_the_lean_is_recovered_from_three_distances` (series built from known `S`, `E`, `L` at 40, 106 and 256 pt fitted back within one grid step) and `test_problems_name_a_lean_on_its_grid_edge`; run `cd packages/mobile && python3 -m unittest tool/glass_lab/harness/tests/test_pressfit.py` (red first).
2. The fit: `fit_lean`, the `lean` command (`pressfit.py lean <series...> --out <json> [--write]`, writing `ios27PressLeanShift`, `ios27PressLeanStretch` and `ios27PressLeanLength` into `ios27_press.dart` beside the table's other constants).
3. Package tests in `glass_press_test.dart`: `a finger dragged outside the glass leans it toward the finger and back on release` (a 250 x 88 interactive glass, the pointer 100 pt past the right edge; after the growth settles `debugGlassPress` reports a centre shift and a ratio change of the fitted size toward the finger, both back to zero after the pointer lifts), `a finger inside the glass does not lean it`, `under Reduce Motion a finger outside the glass does not lean it`.
4. Implement; package and example gates; `material.interactive`'s still check is unaffected (no lean without a drag).
5. `python3 tool/glass_lab/harness/pressfit.py lean $E/references.json $E/press-series.json --out $E/lean.json --write`, `grep PROBLEM` empty, `lab.py build all`, the gates.
6. Commit `feat(ios_liquid_glass): interactive glass leans toward a finger outside it`; the judgement of `drag.{out,up,far}` on `stretch` and `centre` against new native recordings of the scenes the lean was fitted on is in-sample and the results say so. About 3 h, none on the simulator.
