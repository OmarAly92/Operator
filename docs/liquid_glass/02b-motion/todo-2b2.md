# After 2B.2: what is still to do

Date: 2026-10-08. Status: **TODO, not planned yet.** These are the residuals of 2B.2 (branch `feat/ios-liquid-glass-2b2`). Every number and its run folder is in `results-2b2.md`; the per-measure lists are in `research/execution-2b2/analysis-step6.md` (paths below are relative to `research/execution-2b2/`). Classes as in `todo-2b1.md`: **(a)** tunable, **(b)** model limitation or measure that cannot see it, **(c)** a mask or tracker misreads one app, **(d)** capture, **perf**, **defect**.

Where 2B.2 ended: merge, union, morph and the N7 spacing scenes all still have failing measures (Done items 1 to 3), as the plan forecast; Done item 4 passes on eight of the nine still scenes and is **broken on `navbar.inline`** by the user's decision of 2026-10-08 (ruling 33). When an item becomes work it follows ROADMAP §5 and never loosens a limit or a measure.
